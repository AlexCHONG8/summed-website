Key features

Automatic injection, every time

eSimject extends the Simject platform into a reusable form — the same automatic injection sequence, across a full treatment course.

Auto-injector confidence

Needle insertion and injection happen automatically at the press of a button — minimal steps for patients, consistent technique every dose.

Reusable architecture

One durable device across a treatment course, with replaceable drug containers — lower device cost per dose for chronic programs.

Tailored to your URS

Force curves, dose volumes, needle systems, and human factors are configured per program.

OEM development path

Design controls, verification, and regulatory documentation run under our quality system for pharmaceutical sponsors.
