eSimject

Reusable electronic smart auto injector for chronic therapies — automatic injection, connected dosing data, and a ready app-plus-backend platform tailored to your program.

Request specifications
Key features

Automatic injection, connected by design

eSimject extends the Simject platform into a reusable electronic form — the same automatic injection sequence, with dose data your program can build on.

Electronic smart injection

Needle insertion and injection happen automatically at the press of a button — and every dose is logged electronically, so adherence and dose history travel with the device.

eHealthSyne app + backend platform

The companion eHealthSyne app and backend platform are ready to meet customer needs today — with tailor-made configuration an option for programs that need their own data pathways.

Patented connectivity

Smart-pen Bluetooth and 4G communication is covered by a granted China patent — a protected foundation for connected programs.

Reusable architecture

One durable electronic device across a treatment course, with replaceable drug containers — lower device cost per dose for chronic programs.

Tailored to your URS

Specifications are program outputs

eSimject specifications are defined with each program's URS — including how the app and backend integrate with your program. Share your requirements — engineering responds with a configuration and development plan.
